Astartes Gear Upgrade Planner

Plans which hero gear upgrades (imbues with Mithril, mastery with Forgehammers) give the most Expedition power for your resources, based on your gear, your governor stats and your stat priorities. Everything is editable and saved in this browser.

How to use
  1. Your gear: enter the enhancement level (100–200) and mastery (10–20) for all 12 red pieces.
  2. Weights: keep the Default values unless your march is unusual.
  3. Governor stats: copy your Expedition bonuses from the in-game Bonus Overview. Put the Squad bonuses in the first row and each troop's own bonuses below it.
  4. Resources: set what hammers and Mythic gear are worth to you, and enter your stock.
  5. Choose Ideal spend for the long-term order, or Spend all to use up your current stock in the best way.

Your values are saved in this browser only. To move them to another device or send them to a friend, use Export setup and Import setup below.

3 · Governor stats & imbue value

ℹ How stats are valued
The planner uses the multiplicative model, where damage dealt ∝ (1+Atk)(1+Leth) and damage taken ∝ 1 ÷ (1+Def)(1+HP). Each upgrade is scored by how much it raises that troop's offense (Atk × Leth) and survival (Def × HP), Attack and Lethality count equally, and so do Defense and Health. So whichever stat of a pair is lower gets the bigger boost, which always pushes Attack toward Lethality and Defense toward Health. Your totals are updated after every planned step, so returns diminish. The Lethality weight of a troop is used as its offense weight and the Health weight as its survival weight. Conquest bonuses are ignored.

4 · Best bang-for-buck sequence

What resources are worth (in Mithril)

ℹ How to set these
These rates put the three resources into one cost so they can be compared. The defaults use Arena Shop prices: Mithril 10,000 tokens, Forgehammer 2,500, Mythic gear chest 12,000. If hammers are easy for you to get (Bear Hunt and so on), lower the hammer value. If Mythic gear is your bottleneck, raise its value. XP is not counted.

ℹ Ideal spend vs Spend all

Ideal spend ranks every upgrade by value per cost, the order you should follow long-term. With stock entered, the green rows are the upcoming upgrades you can already afford. It stops at the first one you can't.

Spend all builds a plan from your current stock. At each step it takes the highest value-per-cost upgrade that still fits. If the best one is too expensive, it moves on to the next best instead of stopping. This keeps going until nothing fits, so leftovers are as small as possible while the most important upgrades still come first.

Step 0 · Free levels (XP only)

Upgrades, in order

ℹ How to read this table
There are two kinds of upgrade. Mastery adds +1 mastery level, which raises the whole piece's stat by +0.1× base, using hammers and (from M11) Mythic gear. Imbue pays a breakpoint's Mithril and Mythic gear, then levels with XP up to the next cap (e.g. 119 → 139). Mastery and imbue are always separate rows. If an imbue needs a higher mastery (+120 needs M11 and so on), the mastery row comes first, marked "required for next imbue". Value = weighted stat gain + weighted Expedition imbue. Cost = Mithril + hammers + Mythic gear at your rates. Packages: the planner looks ahead along each piece and ranks several steps together when a weak step leads to a strong one.

ℹ Formulas
Gold (0–100): stat = (15 + 0.35 × L) × (1 + 0.1 × M), so +0 is 15% and +100 is 50% before mastery. Red (101–200): stat = (50 + 0.5 × (L − 100)) × (1 + 0.1 × M), so +200 is 100%. Mastery multiplies: M10 = ×2.0, M20 = ×3.0. Mastery Forging unlocks at +20. Mastery level N costs 10 × N Forgehammers, and from M11 it also costs (N − 10) Mythic gear. Ascension at +101 needs +100 and M10 and costs 2 Mythic gear.
